The Journey of a Star

Rajab Butt began his YouTube journey in 2019, initially posting comedic skits and reaction videos. His authentic Punjabi charm and relatable family dynamics quickly resonated with audiences across Pakistan and the diaspora. By 2021, his channel exploded, thanks to viral videos featuring his family, siblings, and everyday adventures.

Known for his collaborative content with other top creators like Ducky Bhai and Sistrology, Rajab has masterfully bridged the gap between humor and heart. His vlogs often feature his mother, father, and siblings, giving fans a genuine look into Pakistani family life. In 2024, he won the "Best Family Vlogger Award" at the Pakistan Digital Content Awards.
